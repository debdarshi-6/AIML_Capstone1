
CAPSTONE PROJECT 8 – CUSTOMER SUPPORT TICKET ROUTING SYSTEM

Total Tickets: 3,000
Primary Key: ticket_id
Target Variables:
- department (5 classes)
- priority (Low/Medium/High)

Files:

1. tickets.csv
- ticket_id: Unique ticket identifier
- ticket_text: Customer issue description
- customer_tenure_months: Customer relationship duration
- previous_tickets_count: Number of previous issues

2. customers.csv
- ticket_id: Foreign key
- customer_region: Region of customer
- account_type: Free/Premium/Enterprise

3. labels.csv
- ticket_id: Foreign key
- department: Target routing department
- priority: Target priority level

Tasks:
- Merge datasets correctly.
- Perform department classification (multi-class).
- Perform priority classification.
- Compare TF-IDF, ANN, and BERT (frozen).
- Benchmark performance and inference time.
